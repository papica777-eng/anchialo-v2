A Legacy Rooted in Ancient Wisdom
Pomorian Black Sea lye and the healing mud from
Pomorie
have been used for therapeutic purposes for over 2,000 years. Ancient Thracian tribes were the first to discover the remarkable healing properties of the region's mineral-rich waters and mud.
During the Roman era (1st-4th century AD), Pomorie gained fame as a balneotherapy hub. The Romans, renowned for their baths and spa culture, embraced Pomorie’s mineral lye and mud for joint pain, skin conditions, and general wellness.
The city of Pomorie, in Bulgaria
Later, under the Ottoman Empire (14th-19th century), Pomorie's therapeutic mud continued to thrive, with local and visiting healers recommending it for rheumatic diseases and skin care.
By the 19th and 20th centuries, Pomorie’s reputation as a health resort flourished, with medical studies validating the benefits of Black Sea lye. Today, these traditional elements remain integral to Bulgarian natural medicine and wellness practices.
