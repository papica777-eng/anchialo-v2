A traditional process that generates not one but two valuable natural products
In Bulgaria, salt has been produced the traditional way for ages, in Burgas with the Atanasovsko salt lake and Pomorie with the Pomorie salt lake. This process generates not one but two extremely valuable natural products.
Everything starts with these unique salt lakes, which contain water much more concentrated in salt than sea water. In addition to their higher salinity, these waters also have a very rich organic and mineral composition.
These saltier water from the lakes are then used to fill smaller salt pans, in which the natural process of evaporation will begin.
Colorful salt pans on the outskirt of Burgas, Bulgaria
Once the evaporation is completed, the salt crystallizes and settles at the bottom of the salt pans, forming a layer of salt of about 10 cm. Just above it, unveils a thick oily liquid with a brownish-red color and a slight odor of hydrogen sulfide, extremely rich in magnesium salts. This is the lye.
The lye being a natural product, its color and smell can vary throughout the year depending on the weather for instance. The lye will mature until it is suitable for medical use, at this moment, it is called medicinal lye and is usually ready between August and September, when it reaches a certain density and quantitative composition of active substances.
But what is Black Sea lye made of?
The unique healing properties of the Black Sea lye come from its rich mineral and organic composition. It contains all the ingredients of seawater and lake water, but in higher proportions.
In terms of composition, the lye is close to the healing Pomorie estuarine mud, which is extracted from the lake of the same name, and that presents similar effect and healing properties. The red oily lye sitting above crystallized salt in a salt pans in Bulgaria. Lye contains many elements such as magnesium, calcium, sodium, chlorine, bromine, sulfates, bicarbonates, hydrocarbons, trace elements and organic substances.
The red oily lye sitting above crystallized salt in a salt pans in Bulgaria.
The main ingredient is magnesium salts - magnesium chloride (MgCl2) and magnesium sulfate (MgSO4). Magnesium is an extremely important element that plays important roles in the human body. From the production of energy in cells, to the balance of the nervous and muscular systems, the reduction of muscle pain and prevention of cramps, it also has beneficial effects on hair, skin and nails.
Stress and fatigue, both physical or mental, drain magnesium from the cells in the body. Therefore, after physical or mental stress, the body needs to get enough of this valuable mineral for our body. In addition to its rich mineral composition, the lye also contains many biologically active substances such as microalgae that are rich in chlorophyll and vitamins and zooplankton and bacteria, which are characteristic of the waters of our salt lakes.
Before it can be used, the Black Sea lye must be stabilized
The Black Sea lye that you see in the salt pans is almost ready, but there is one last step before it can be used in cosmetics. After filtering the lye from the pool, in which it was stored, it must be filtered and stabilized within 24 hours. Otherwise, it would lose too much of its valuable healing properties.
