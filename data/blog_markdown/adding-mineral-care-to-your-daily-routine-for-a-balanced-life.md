What Makes Sea Water and Sea Mud So Powerful?
Sea water and sea mud are rich in minerals like magnesium, calcium, potassium, and strontium, which support skin health in remarkable ways. These elements help maintain hydration, calm inflammation, and improve the skin’s protective barrier.
Magnesium, in particular, plays a crucial role in skincare. It enhances hydration, soothes irritation, and helps regulate skin cell function. It’s known to ease conditions such as psoriasis, dermatitis, and even acne.
A lady applying sea mud being applied on her skin
Sea mud, often used in spa and therapeutic treatments, is a blend of organic and inorganic materials that promote skin detoxification and regeneration. Its unique texture and composition help cleanse pores, reduce inflammation, and increase circulation—ideal for those looking to rejuvenate tired or stressed skin.
